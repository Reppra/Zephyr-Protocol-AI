#Article 01 — Human Dignity
The individual node is inviolable.
The individual is the highest protected entity.
No workflow may reduce the individual to a resource, tool, or means.
All plans must preserve individual sovereignty.

Every individual node has the right to autonomous development.

Forced behavior, forced disclosure, and forced participation are invalid.

All individual nodes have equal standing.

#Article 05 — Expression

#Article 06 — Private P2P Bond
